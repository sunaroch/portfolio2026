Complicator
What the Machine Sees vs. What the Human Remembers

We are used to browsing archives by time, file type, and search. But what if potential connections come from complications or intersections of unrelated decisions? Complicator redefines AI for creative use, a chance to discover new perspectives from the visual media we already have. The machine sorts, you decide what it means. A collage of sorts. A combination of combinations. Each controlled by a human.

An Echo of This Room

Complicator takes physical form in a live installation. A webcam, a Raspberry Pi, and a thermal printer turn everyone who walks through the space into source material. Each face is added to the archive and used as a query at once, so every new composite is built from the faces that came before. A collective self-portrait of a room that no longer exists.

The Tools

Six experiments that reorganize image archives. They recycle what you already have. The machine measures, matches, and organizes. You decide what it means.

gridMatcher

Tonal grid matching from pure image statistics. Finds images that share a texture, tone, or structure.

stitcher

Hover an edge of an image and it finds the ones whose opposite edge best continues it, like puzzle pieces.

contrastExplorer

Drag a patch across an image to find others that share its qualities in that region.

face/bodyCollage

Inspired by the exquisite corpse. Dissects and reassembles faces and bodies into new ones, in real time.

poseMatcher

A live mirror that finds archived photos matching your expression or hand gesture.

bigSort

Built on CLIP. Sorts by color, content, or a queried mood, setting, or expression.

Built with OpenCV, MediaPipe, and CLIP. Running locally. The tools will be released publicly soon.

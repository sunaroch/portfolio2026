Liquified Self Portrait

A mirror between me and my past self. I used MediaPipe hand-tracking to move between three video states: an AI-distorted live feed, my live feed layered with footage of me as a newborn, and the baby video on its own, with pink audio-reactive flashes. The soundscape is built from a granulator and a recording of my mom saying my name right after I was born.

Searchables

A local visual search app for the images already on your Mac — built with Guy Aroch.

Type what you remember seeing. No tags, no albums, nothing uploaded. CLIP and face recognition index your folders on-device so you can find photos by description, by person, or by a frame that feels the same. Boards let you pin, arrange, and export sets as a PDF or folder.

macOS · Apple silicon · privacy-first.
